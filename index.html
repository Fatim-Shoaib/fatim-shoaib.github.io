Selected research & publications

Controlled, mechanistic studies of how modern models actually behave — from federated learning to vision-language hallucination and diffusion quantization.

◆ ICML 2026 Workshop · Mechanistic Interpretability

Preserved-but-Misaligned Representations in Non-IID FedAvg

Co-authored study showing that FedAvg's accuracy collapse under non-IID data is representation misalignment, not forgetting: class-specific circuits survive aggregation, but the global classifier head can no longer read them. Evidence via L0-sparse circuit identification, a Universal Sparse Autoencoder, linear probing, and SVCCA geometry.

~0%end-to-end acc. for collapsed classes…
recoverable…yet circuits stay highly functional
SVCCAshared sparse basis, IID vs non-IID

◆ Vision-Language · Independent study + code

Whitening Rotates, Architecture Mediates

A readout-alignment account of how CLIP feature whitening affects object hallucination in connector-only VLMs. ZCA whitening cuts hallucination for a Q-Former but worsens it for MLP connectors — localized to decision-margin geometry, not perception.

◆ Edge AI · Industry collaboration (10xEngineers)

Log-SNR & Hessian-Based Diffusion Quantization

Dynamic activation quantization keyed on log-SNR instead of raw timestep, plus Hessian-based 4-bit weight PTQ (GPTQ / Qronos) for diffusion transformers — near-FP16 fidelity at a fraction of the size.

About

Where research meets shipping

I hold a BSc Computer Science (specialization in Artificial Intelligence) from LUMS (graduated June 2026), and I'm an AI Research Engineer I at Skylabs AI (promoted from AI Intern). My work lives at the intersection of applied AI research and engineering — I like building systems that actually run, then digging into why they behave the way they do.

At Skylabs I'm currently building efficient distributed, federated-style LLM training across heterogeneous cloud backends (in-house servers, AWS, RunPod) using DeepMind's DiLoCo low-communication algorithm. Before that I researched pruning-based LLM compression at LUMS and image-captioning architectures at GIST's Machine Learning & Computer Vision Lab in South Korea. Across coursework and research I've implemented DDPM, RLHF (PPO/DPO/GRPO/RLVR), knowledge distillation, and VLM connectors from scratch — and co-authored work accepted at an ICML 2026 workshop.
